## Analysis Questions

### 1. Which cloud provider offers the broadest range of services?
**AWS** offers the broadest range of services with over 200 fully-featured services. As the oldest and largest cloud provider, AWS has had the most time to develop and expand its service portfolio, making it suitable for virtually any workload.

### 2. Which provider best integrates with Microsoft technologies?
**Microsoft Azure** provides the best integration with Microsoft technologies, offering native support for Windows Server, Active Directory, Microsoft 365, .NET Framework, and SQL Server. This makes Azure the natural choice for organizations with existing Microsoft investments.

### 3. Which provider is strongest in Artificial Intelligence and Kubernetes?
**Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** excels in both AI/ML and Kubernetes. Kubernetes was developed at Google, and GKE (Google Kubernetes Engine) is considered the industry standard. GCP's AI and machine learning tools, including TensorFlow and Vertex AI, are world-class.

### 4. Which cloud platform would you personally choose and why?
I would choose **AWS** because of its market leadership, extensive documentation, and proven reliability. AWS's massive ecosystem of tools, partners, and community support makes it the safest choice for most applications. While other providers have specific strengths, AWS's versatility is unmatched.
