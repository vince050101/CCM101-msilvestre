# Mission Reflection

## My Cloud Platform Impressions

**Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** impressed me the most. Despite being the "third" cloud provider, GCP's focus on data analytics, AI/ML, and Kubernetes shows how innovation can differentiate a platform. Google's infrastructure, which powers billions of users daily, gives GCP unique technical advantages.

## Similarities Among AWS, Azure, and GCP

All three cloud platforms share fundamental similarities:
- Core services: Computing, storage, networking, and identity
- Global infrastructure with regions and availability zones
- Pay-as-you-go pricing models
- Management consoles and CLI tools
- IAM for security and access control

## How Business Requirements Influence Cloud Selection

Business requirements are the primary driver for cloud platform selection. Technical advantages mean nothing if they don't align with a company's needs, existing investments, and strategic goals. Cost, compliance, integration, and specific technical requirements must all be considered.

## Lessons from Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant

I learned that being a cloud architect requires both technical knowledge and business acumen. Understanding each platform's strengths and weaknesses is essential for making recommendations that truly solve problems. The goal isn't to promote a favorite provider but to find the best fit for each unique scenario.
